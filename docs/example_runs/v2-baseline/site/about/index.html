About

Independent. Specific. Useful.

singletenant.ai is an independent buyer's resource for single-tenant LLM hosting — written for engineering leaders, procurement teams, and compliance officers shipping AI inside boundaries that matter.

The category is real but the buyer side of the market is poorly served. Vendor pages oversell. Analyst reports lag the technology. Reddit threads are rumour. We exist to fill the gap with regularly- updated vendor profiles, honest comparisons, and analysis grounded in real procurement and operational experience.

Edited by Simon Wilson

I run the editorial here. My background: seven years leading global product delivery at Ocado Technology, where I shipped infrastructure rollouts across multiple territories under operational and compliance constraints; before that, the British Army and reconstruction programmes in Iraq.

I now consult on AI delivery for compliance-heavy buyers — financial services, healthcare, defence, public sector — where the question is rarely "which model is best" and almost always "which deployment architecture clears legal, security, and procurement at the same time".

Editorial principles

Vendor neutrality. Some links on this site earn referral commission; we always disclose this clearly on the relevant pages. Commission never determines coverage. Our directory includes vendors with no partnership relationship.

Data first. Every vendor profile is grounded in structured data we maintain ourselves. We cite sources, mark review dates, and update profiles when material changes happen.

Specific, not generic. "Best LLM hosting for 2026" listicles aren't useful. We write for the buyer with a defined deployment context — a regulated industry, a compliance regime, a specific scale of workload. If a piece could apply to anyone, we haven't done our job.
