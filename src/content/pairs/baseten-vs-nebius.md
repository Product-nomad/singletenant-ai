These are two dedicated-inference platforms under different law.
[Nebius](/vendors/nebius/) contracts non-US, non-Israeli customers through
Nebius B.V. under Dutch law, per its [services agreement](https://docs.nebius.com/legal/agreement).
[Baseten](/vendors/baseten/) contracts as Baseten Labs, Inc. under its
[terms](https://www.baseten.co/terms-and-conditions/), a US company.

An EU parent is still not EU sovereignty. The same Nebius agreement moves US
customers who registered for Token Factory on or after 15 September 2026 to
Nebius Inc., and Nebius makes no sovereignty claim on its
[enterprise inference page](https://nebius.com/services/token-factory/enterprise-grade-inference). This site records it as EU-parented,
not sovereign for the EU.

Both let you choose a region, with different backing. Nebius's
[Data Processing Agreement](https://docs.nebius.com/legal/dpa) (section 6.1) commits dedicated
endpoints to the region the customer selects, a Contractual commitment.
Baseten's [regional deployments](https://docs.baseten.co/deployment/regional-deployments) let GPU model deployments select a
region, with US and European Union among the values; chains, training jobs and
shared CPU instances cannot. That is a documented product control, so it is
Stated.

On cost, Baseten [bills](https://www.baseten.co/pricing) compute down to the minute. Nebius bills
a dedicated endpoint [while at least one replica is running](https://docs.tokenfactory.nebius.com/ai-models-inference/dedicated-endpoints/billing-policy);
its dedicated rates are not verified here.
