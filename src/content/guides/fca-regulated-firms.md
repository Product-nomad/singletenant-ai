## Who this guide is for

- Firms regulated by the FCA, and dual-regulated firms also supervised by the PRA.
- Not legal advice. Say so plainly and point to the firm's own compliance function.

## Outsourcing and third-party risk

- The FCA's outsourcing requirements (SYSC 8) and the PRA's supervisory statement on outsourcing and third-party risk management: what they ask of a firm adopting hosted inference.
- Notification expectations for material outsourcing.
- Contract terms the rules point to: audit and access rights, subcontracting, termination.

## Operational resilience

- Important business services and impact tolerances: when an LLM-backed service falls in scope.
- Exit planning and stressed exit: what single tenancy changes, and what it does not.
- Concentration risk with hyperscaler-hosted capacity.

## The critical third parties regime

- What the regime covers and why it does not remove a firm's own obligations.

## Data protection and transfers

- UK GDPR controller and processor roles for prompts and outputs.
- International transfers and the role of parent jurisdiction (link the jurisdiction article).

## Model risk

- Where model risk management expectations apply to LLM use, and what a hosting choice contributes (versioning, change notification).

## Using the matrix

- Fields that map to these questions: residency guarantee, parent jurisdiction, SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001.
- What the matrix cannot tell a firm (contract terms, audit rights), so what to ask the vendor.
