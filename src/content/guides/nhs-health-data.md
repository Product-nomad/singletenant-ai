## Who this guide is for

- NHS trusts, integrated care boards and suppliers in England.
- Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland: flag the differences, do not assume.

## Health data is special category data

- UK GDPR Article 9 and the conditions for processing; the common law duty of confidentiality; the Caldicott Principles.

## Assurance a buyer will be asked for

- The Data Security and Protection Toolkit (DSPT): who must complete it and at what standard.
- The Digital Technology Assessment Criteria (DTAC) for digital health technologies.
- Clinical safety standards DCB0129 (manufacturer) and DCB0160 (deploying organisation), and when an LLM feature triggers them.

## Where data may be hosted

- Current NHS guidance on cloud hosting and on processing data outside the UK; record the guidance version and date.
- Residency guarantee versus parent jurisdiction for health data (link the residency vs sovereignty guide).

## Why HIPAA appears in the matrix

- A HIPAA BAA is a US instrument. It is useful evidence of a vendor's health-data controls but is not a UK requirement or an equivalent.

## Using the matrix

- Fields to read first: UK region, residency guarantee, parent jurisdiction, ISO 27001.
- Questions the matrix cannot answer: DSPT status, clinical safety documentation, contract terms.

## Sources to cite

- NHS England (DSPT, DTAC, clinical safety), DHSC, ICO.
