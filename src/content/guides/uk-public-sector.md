## Who this guide is for

- Central government departments, arm's-length bodies and local authorities.
- NHS bodies have their own guide; point to it rather than duplicating it.

## Questions to settle before shortlisting

- The classification of the workload's data under the Government Security Classifications, and what that allows for hosted inference.
- Where data may be processed and stored, and who may access it (staff location, support access, subprocessors).
- Whether the workload needs single tenancy at all, or whether a shared endpoint with contractual controls meets the requirement.

## Procurement routes

- Which Crown Commercial Service frameworks and G-Cloud lots cover hosted inference, and how to check the current iteration.
- What changed for buyers under the Procurement Act 2023.
- When a call-off or direct award is possible, and what evidence a buyer should keep.

## Assurance

- Which certifications buyers commonly ask for (Cyber Essentials Plus, ISO 27001) and how they map to the matrix compliance column.
- The NCSC Cloud Security Principles as a checklist for dedicated hosting.
- What a vendor's trust centre does and does not evidence.

## Sovereignty and jurisdiction

- Residency is not sovereignty. Link the UK buyer's position article and the residency vs sovereignty guide.
- The UK-sovereign category in the matrix, and why it is short.
