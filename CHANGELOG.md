## v2.4 reposition as an independent reference (2026-10-17, branch `reposition/2026-10`, PR open)

Turns the site from a reference site with a consulting funnel into an independent reference site, prepares monetisation surfaces that stay switched off, and acts on the findings in `AUDIT.md`. These are site, governance and schema changes, so they are recorded here only. No vendor fact changed, so nothing is added to the public `/changelog/`. Adding `last_verified` and `sources` re-recorded existing data and verified nothing new.

- 2026-10-17: Audit (`AUDIT.md`). Covers self-promotion, thin and duplicate content, missing dates and broken links per page; a distinctiveness score for each of the 120 compare pairs; vendor freshness (every fact is more than 60 days old); and a structured-data review. Adds `npm run linkcheck` (internal links always; `--external` for outbound).
- 2026-10-17: Editorial trust. Consulting and services copy removed everywhere (ConsultingCTA deleted; nav, footer, /contact/ and /about/ rewritten). New `/editorial-policy/` (sourcing, verification, corrections, conflicts), linked from the footer and /methodology/. `/disclosure/` covers advertising and sponsorship as well as referrals: none of them influences the matrix, rankings or inclusion, and sponsored content is always labelled. The methodology's category count was corrected from four to five.

- 2026-10-17: Vendor re-verification. All 87 facts across 16 vendors re-checked against the rendered text of their primary sources; all still supported. ## v2.2 (2026-07-13, branch `main`)

- 2026-07-13: Structured data (JSON-LD) audited and extended across the vendor and compare page templates. Shared builders now live in `src/lib/schema.mjs` and emit, per page: the `/vendors/` directory as a `Dataset` plus an `ItemList` enumerating all 15 vendors; each vendor profile as an enriched `SoftwareApplication` (now carrying a `provider` Organization and a `dateModified` from its latest verified fact) plus a `BreadcrumbList`; and each of the 105 comparison pages as a `Dataset` whose `about` is the two vendors' `SoftwareApplication`s, plus a `BreadcrumbList`. The comparison pages previously carried no structured data at all. Only facts the pages already render are emitted, no ratings, reviews, or prices; `tests/jsonld.test.mjs` enforces that integrity rule in code. This earns Google Dataset Search and rich-result eligibility and gives AI engines a clean, citable data graph. Rich Results Test validation against the live URLs is browser-gated and deferred to Simon, alongside the outstanding Lighthouse checks.

- 2026-07-13: Article pages moved onto the same shared builders. `ArticleLayout` now calls `articleSchema` (in `src/lib/schema.mjs`) instead of an inline object, emitting the post as an `Article` (headline, `author` Person, `datePublished`/`dateModified`) plus a Home > Blog > title `BreadcrumbList`. The `publisher` Organization now carries the site `url` + `logo` it was missing, matching every other template, and the same forbidden-keys test guards the article graph.
